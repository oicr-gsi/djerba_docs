Contact Us
==========

Djerba is developed and maintained by the Clinical Genome Interpretation (CGI) team at the `Ontario Institute for Cancer Research`_.

To contact the Djerba developers, email `gsi@oicr.on.ca`_ or open an `issue on GitHub`_.

CGI's main responsibility is clinical reporting for cancer research at OICR, and our capacity to address Djerba issues other than production-critical bugfixes is limited. That said, we welcome use of Djerba by external collaborators, and will do our best to advise on good practice and troubleshooting. Suggestions on how to improve Djerba and its documentation are also welcome.

.. _Ontario Institute for Cancer Research: https://oicr.on.ca/
.. _gsi@oicr.on.ca: mailto:gsi@oicr.on.ca
.. _issue on Github: https://github.com/oicr-gsi/djerba/issues
